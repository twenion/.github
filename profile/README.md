## How we work

- Start with a verifiable problem, not a technology label.
- Keep scope explicit, limited, and measurable.
- Show working prototypes when they make the proposed change easier to evaluate.
- Measure before and after where the available data makes that honest.
- Leave code, domains, and business accounts under the client's control.
